Sensor Hardware Specifications

Microcontroller pin configurations and transducer calibration details

TMP117 Clinical Temperature ACTIVE

I2C Interface: SDA Pin D4 / SCL Pin D5 (Address 0x48)

Parameters: High-Precision Patient Body Temperature (°C)

Accuracy: ±0.1°C (-20°C to +50°C, meets ASTM E1112)

Sampling Period: 1.0 Second

Firmware Setpoint: Early warning buzzer trigger at > 34.0 °C

MPU6050 6-Axis Motion / Posture ACTIVE

I2C Interface: SDA Pin D4 / SCL Pin D5 (Address 0x68)

Parameters: Posture Vector, Orientation Angle (°), 3-Axis Accel & Gyro

Scale: ±2g Accelerometer, ±250 °/s Gyroscope

Early Warning Logic: Absolute orientation > 90° triggers bed-exit alert

MAX30102 Pulse Oximeter & Heart Rate STANDBY

I2C Interface: SDA Pin D4 / SCL Pin D5 (Address 0x57)

Optical Sensing: Red & Infrared LEDs + Photodetector for PPG

Physical State: Displays disconnected when physically absent

Telemetry Output: Heart Rate (BPM) emitted when sensor attached

Hardware Alarm Buzzer ACTIVE

ESP32 Pin: GPIO 25 (Digital Output, Active HIGH)

Trigger Logic: if (temp > 34 || abs(orient) > 90) digitalWrite(25, HIGH);

Web Integration: Alerts trigger strictly on BUZZER: ON ...

Clear Logic: Dismisses strictly on BUZZER: OFF
